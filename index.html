PhD Computer Science · Associate Professor, Dept. of Computer Engineering · Researcher, SPI&FM Group

Biography

I received my MSc in Computer Science and my PhD degree at the University of Cádiz, where I work as an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Engineering and as a researcher at the Software Process Improvement and Formal Methods group (SPI&FM).

My research is focused on the field of Technology-Enhanced Learning (TEL), with a particular interest in learning analytics and the implementation and use of conversational agents in higher education. Previously, I worked as a project manager in different Spanish IT companies.
